# TXA Files
 TXA Files are a lot harder of a format to manage. Due to this, unlike other files, TXA files won't pack themselves from the main editor. Instead, they have their own dedicated editor.
#### 
## TXA Editor
 You can make and edit TXA files from scratch in this editor, since I figure it's the best way to handle animation editing. You can pack new textures in and be done with it, but you can also add, remove, and replace frames. There are two "Length" values, but you'll have to fiddle with them to get it into a nice state. I can't actually determine how fast it will play ingame without further testing, hence, this is in beta.

 Since this is in beta, and am currently writing this without having even made the editor, I'm mostly just setting goals. The number one goal with this is to make Riku's face play Bad Apple when trying to blink.
#### 
### Saving Files
 You can save files in two different ways. There's project files, which are not packed, but keep everything as it was when you closed the window, and TXA files, which pack your work into a TXA file, which loses some info about what textures you linked and a couple other things. I recommend saving both.
